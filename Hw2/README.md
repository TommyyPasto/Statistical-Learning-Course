# Homework 2: Kernel Density Estimation

Nonparametric density estimation with kernels, focused on the choice of the bandwidth h and the bias-variance trade-off.

- **Methods compared:** Silverman's rule of thumb and cross-validation for selecting h
- **Data:** two simulated datasets built as mixtures of Normal and Gamma distributions, with multiple modes and different scales
